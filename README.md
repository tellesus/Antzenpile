A colony-scale strategy game about interpreting and shaping a living ant trail network. The player is the colony: the physical world is hidden, and play proceeds through incomplete collective knowledge and an abstract audiovisual sensorium.

## Running and testing

Download and extract the standard Windows x64 ZIP from the [official 4.7.2 archive](https://godotengine.org/download/archive/4.7.2-stable/) or its [official GitHub mirror](https://github.com/godotengine/godot-builds/releases/tag/4.7.2-stable). The engine is not checked into the repository. In PowerShell, set `GODOT_EXE` to the extracted console executable, then run from the repository root:

```powershell
$env:GODOT_EXE = 'C:\path\to\Godot_v4.7.2-stable_win64_console.exe'
& $env:GODOT_EXE --version
& $env:GODOT_EXE --headless --path . --import
& $env:GODOT_EXE --headless --path . --script res://tests/run_tests.gd
& $env:GODOT_EXE --headless --path . --quit-after 3
& $env:GODOT_EXE --editor --path .
```

The runner returns 0 on success, 1 on check failure or an empty suite. Use the pinned executable, not an arbitrary Godot on PATH. In the editor, F5 runs Main and F8 stops it. The near-black OUTWARD window uses a 1280×720 landscape viewport and canvas-items stretch. Drag the field with a mouse or touch to face another direction; tap a trace for its context. Once a scout has returned with a known resource, its context offers **Assign 5 Gatherers**, then −1, +1 and Cancel. If a trail reports an unavailable source, select it and use **Try Gathering** after its travelers return for an uncertain trip, or **Resume Gathering** after a confirming report returns. **Watch and Resume Gathering** uses standing exploration to confirm renewal and resume existing gathering; **Stop Recovery Watch** removes that intent. A selected known trace offers **Prioritize Source Rechecks** / **Stop Priority Rechecks**; it shares standing exploration labor and queues while effort is off. A returned honeydew trace can be harvested by a trail; after a loaded return, **Tend Producers** commits six available workers at baseline (recognition expression changes newly assigned staffing), and **Withdraw Tenders** releases them. A dangerous honeydew trail can lose workers; an ALARM trace and loss count appear only after return or a missed expected return. **Recall gatherers** blocks departures and recalls survivors; invest those workers in a different known source to avoid the threat. A returned FOREIGN trace can escalate to contested contact; +4 commits reinforcements that still travel to the crossing. Recall gatherers withdraws survivors, and their outcome arrives only at home. Lost workers are not automatically replaced. Loaded returns make a thin screen-space scent link appear; disuse breaks and fades it. Recall blocks departures and returns workers to availability only when their cohort reaches home. The **Exploration** button opens Off/2/5/8 effort controls. **Favor This Direction** saves an explicit bias; turning the panorama only changes what you look at. General exploration remains part of the mix. Scouts follow sensed cues, bring partial/empty accounts home after bounded searches, and are replaced when available labor and shared capacity permit. Lowering effort recalls surplus scouts through actual travel. Some general explorers follow established trails and search beyond their endpoints. Investigation priorities revisit uncertain or depleted known sources without repeated launch clicks; gathering rechecks remain a separate resource-trail action. Private evidence appears only after arrival. Use INWARD or Tab to switch to the abstract colony network. Select the violet Adaptation Web for a Lean or Load brood trial once its displayed costs and Nursery space are available; the new trait takes effect as adapted brood emerges. After returned wet-trail experience, newly laid brood may reveal a Persistent Trail Chemistry candidate; its selected context offers a separate chemistry brood trial. After foreign/partner experience and surviving brood variation, **Inspect Recognition** opens the security/tolerance family; inspect either leaf and use its one contextual brood-trial action. Reassign producer tending to apply newly expressed staffing. Only one focused trial can grow at a time, and existing adults retain their traits. Selecting Adaptation focuses its web: violet genetic leaves show trial/expression, and a green Honeydew relationship leaf appears after returned source evidence. Harvesting enables worker-funded tending there as well as in OUTWARD; Colony Network closes web attention. After brood emerges, select Queen or Nursery and tap Lay 8 Brood to begin another cycle. Select Nursery to develop it when the displayed costs and labor are available; once complete, Lay 8 Brood can start a second cohort before the first emerges. After a guest enters, INWARD adds a Guest function. At baseline, Nursery losses first have uncertain cause, then repeated losses reveal internal foreignness; security associates harm earlier and tolerance later. Assign 4 to Rejection commits four workers until purge; Stop Rejection releases them, and the guest can remain harmful during clearing. A later fresh guest starts with new local symptoms; prior losses remain in colony history, and no arrival forecast is shown. Select Food Exchange and, once stores meet its displayed requirements, tap Develop Food Exchange; its context shows progress and the completed benefit. Each view remembers its selection. Pause/Resume and 1×/4×/16×/64× buttons also have Space and 1/2/3/4 shortcuts. Save and Load buttons in either view (or Ctrl+S/Ctrl+L) use one local slot at `user://saves/slot_1.json`; loading resets view selection/facing and music phase while restoring the simulation. F3 toggles DEBUG: WORLD TRUTH; click home, resource, or scout markers for raw fields, including brood, development, trail chemistry and commitments. Signals remain absent until a scout returns. Release and headless runs do not create the truth view.
