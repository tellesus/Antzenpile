**Current development save policy (2026-10-15):** [Same-build saves only](../SAVE_POLICY.md). Earlier requirements to preserve/load/migrate previous-build saves are superseded and tabled until true release preparation. Historical implementation/evidence below does not create a compatibility goal.

Current 1.0 design. Shared rules: [SYS-10](../SYSTEMS_BIBLE.md#sys-10-ecology-encounters-and-conflict), [SYS-11](../SYSTEMS_BIBLE.md#sys-11-weather-local-health-and-disturbance). Delivery: [M6](../ROADMAP_1_0.md), with physical rescue in M5 first. Creature profiles are game archetypes inspired by biology, not literal species simulations.

## Four rule families

| Family | Common behavior | Common counterplay |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Partner/producer | Physical food production and an optional worker-supported relationship. | Gather, tend, reduce service, withdraw and choose another source. Tenders do not become defenders. |
| Competitor | Independent aggregate labor seeks food, meets physical traffic, commits finite reserves and returns/reports. | Reinforce gathering, withdraw, share/avoid geography and investigate an alternate approach. |
| Huntable predator | Physical position/movement, encounter range, attack/retreat/resistance and optional finite edible result. | Investigate, choose Clear/Hunt, commit/reinforce/retreat and avoid. A kill differs from displacement. |
| Unassailable disturbance | Physical zone/corridor/local exposure changes travel, scent, food or nest condition. | Stop/recall, reroute, isolate, prepare refuge or evacuate. Sending a larger fighting force does not solve it. |

Guests and weather use separate local-health/environment owners but connect to those strategic roles. “Encounter” does not imply a menu battle: aggregate cohorts, scouts and physical zones cause events; reports make evidence known afterward.

### Content template

Each entry supplies: stable ID and role; actual trigger/position or movement family; targets/exposure; state transitions; finite population/resistance/production budget; witness cues and recognition stages; eligible actions and costs; settlement/renewal; save ownership and one complete warning→action→returned result→recovery case. No content entry can bypass worker loss ownership or read hidden truth into UI.

Movement is a bounded physical behavior with seeded decisions, not an individual-entity ecosystem simulation. A relocation updates actual swept intersections and later knowledge; a creature cannot teleport to punish a chosen button. Newly authored content may share an encounter controller only where state/settlement genuinely match.

### Partners and rivals

Aphid output is owned by the physical producer. Service can improve new output; it does not refund harvesting or teleport food. Recognition affects service/guest costs through its defined hooks, not the rival's world identity.

Rivals need enough economic continuity to remain relevant beyond one encounter, but do not need the player's full queen/genetics UI or empire planner. If replacement workers are added, a bounded aggregate food-funded production rule must debit its real independent economy; no invisible unlimited replenishment. Goals can be forage, defend an encountered junction and withdraw depleted commitments. Changing rival timing/reserves requires ordinary failed/withdrawn/reinforced comparisons, not arbitrary retaliation.

### Predator movement and settlement

Separate position/movement from combat. Crossing a predator's actual encounter area causes the attack owner to debit real ants. A clear result changes its actual occupation/pressure; hunting requires the additional physical kill result before creating remains. Roaming beetle behavior uses a small corridor/pause state with deterministic saved decisions. Old sightings remain dated rather than following its new position.

Displacement may resolve the immediate route while another location later receives new returned evidence. Never reuse an old encounter receipt to cancel a fresh order. A cleared/carcass source does not promise permanent safety from other organisms.

### Disturbance, warning and rescue

Tainted ground, heavy passage and ground disturbance have physical footprints/exposure, not global damage to all colonies. Types specify affected cargo/segments/occupants. A connection closure must identify which real party is still committed; the same queen/worker/cargo cannot appear at both ends.

Warnings arise from an actual local cue or a returning witness. A response window is an authored design property of the hazard, not a forecast UI of its private timetable. Refuge mitigates the supported exposure while evacuation moves assets over a real connection. Deliberate abandonment records what was transported, lost or left; it never wipes hazards or issues a free replacement queen.

### Recognition stages

Unknown loss/cue → witnessed role/scale → supported physical impression → specific profile identity where evidence warrants it. A generic impact does not become “dog” through elapsed time alone. A dog-passage entry must supply actual supported cues; old generic Roadside saves retain generic disturbance. Body outlines/labels should clarify response eligibility without revealing live health, exact strength or a future path.
