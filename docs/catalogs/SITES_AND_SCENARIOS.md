# Nest sites and scenario catalog

Current 1.0 design. Shared rules: [SYS-09](../SYSTEMS_BIBLE.md#sys-09-sites-nests-and-network-transport), [SYS-12](../SYSTEMS_BIBLE.md#sys-12-scenario-chapters-viability-and-endings). Delivery: [M5/M7](../ROADMAP_1_0.md). Existing settings remain Backyard, Garden Edge and Roadside.

## Site rules family

A site definition declares supported physical shelter, damp/thermal exposure, preparation effort, connection access and occupancy risk. A site instance owns actual location/occupation/condition. A returned site memory has estimated location and witnessed properties; it never guarantees safety or reveals nearby unseen resources. Founding pays for real travel/preparation and learns its result through a returning messenger.

Site descriptions are game profiles, not universal geological claims. Different profiles need a practical choice, not a hidden scoring bonus. Discovery says what was sensed (“sheltered opening”, “damp ground”), not an omniscient list of complete modifiers. After occupation, locally observable conditions can be inspected normally.

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Stable ID / visible cues | Profile identity learned from evidence; old generic shelter memories remain valid. |
| Shelter / moisture / thermal bands | Bounded exposure/regulation differences that use SYS-11; no immunity or free water generation. |
| Preparation / initial supported capacity | Paid effort and starting functional condition; worker/brood capacities still come from actual organs. |
| Access / connection constraints | Actual segment length/terrain/closure; no straight-line teleport transport. |
| Occupancy / hazard hooks | Physical occupants or disturbance eligibility, revealed by investigation/occupation. |
| Scenario placement | Authored/seeded instance distribution that admits viable alternatives and a multi-pile network. |
| Migration/knowledge checks | Stable profile version, arrival/occupation ownership and delayed evidence. |

This is an acceptance example, not a hard arbitrary three-pile cap.

## Scenario rules family

A scenario composes source profiles, terrain/exposure, site instances, bounded ecology roles, initial funded colony state and authored event families. It does not need a separate simulation engine or unique interface. Seeds vary stochastic physical decisions and the specifically declared placement/events; preserve saved worlds rather than rebuilding them from new content files.

Every launch setting supports establishment, worker growth, at least one useful trait pressure, paid reproductives, expansion and a recoverable crisis. Success is biological continuity/viable accomplishment; continued play remains available. Outcomes do not depend on an arbitrary territory score, resource grant or mandatory elapsed-time ending.

### SC-BACKYARD — Backyard

**Foundation layout; complete arc planned M7.** Introduce the basic nutrient/return loop with recognizable nectar/insect remains/puddle profiles and at least one food alternative after depletion. Aphids and an ambush predator provide a service-versus-risk decision. A sheltered soil site and a different connection/site profile let expansion change access and resilience. A later witnessed threat/heat/resource change tests recovery without requiring an unknown advanced action during the opening.

Required choices: scouting versus immediate gathering; Home growth versus reproductive/site investment; clear/hunt/avoid; practical food/water contingency. Verify a continued reproductive lineage, including a recoverable shortage and a real saved return. It need not show every catalog entry at once.

### SC-GARDEN — Garden Edge

**Foundation layout; complete arc planned M7.** Emphasize nectar/honeydew relationships, fruit/seed opportunities, moisture differences and water discovery. Root/stone sites offer distinct care/access costs. Climate/care traits and the new organs have a plausible pressure; a roaming hunter changes the usefulness of old route memories. More food production never guarantees sufficient care or protein.

Required choices: attendants versus gatherers, care/adaptation versus new capacity, dependable source versus temporary mixed food, local specialization versus transport. Verify three-pile independent work and daughter-origin reproductives/founding under real supply constraints.

### SC-ROADSIDE — Roadside

**Foundation layout; complete arc planned M7.** Emphasize exposed temporary crumbs, meaningful water alternatives, heavy passage and suspect intake/ground. Sites differ in access/shelter rather than handing out free combat or reserves. A nest/connection disturbance appears only when usable warning/isolating/refuge/evacuation actions exist; the opening must have a discoverable viable route.

Required choices: exploit temporary abundance versus safer food; fight a manageable predator versus avoid unassailable exposure; isolate suspect traffic while incoming cargo remains real; evacuate a viable lineage versus hold a prepared nest. Verify both successful rescue and genuinely unrecoverable loss without premature hidden-truth notification.

## Integration evidence, not brute-force content testing

Use a few purposeful command-driven cases per setting and player checks for their decisions. Cover opening scarcity, full Nursery/reproductive queue, one relevant new trait, an interrupted supply/migration, a returned danger and post-crisis growth. Reuse saved twins at meaningful boundaries; do not run every permutation of sources/traits/sites merely because the catalogs make combinations possible.

The release matrix must cover all ten resource/trait profiles across the three scenarios, four site profiles, a paid queen generation, multi-pile ownership and every ecological role. A successful scenario proves its choices and recovery paths, not a fixed 25–30 minute session. Performance scales are measured separately on stated hardware.

Post 1.0: campaign destinations, comprehensive annual seasons, many more authored environments, huge nest empires and arbitrary procedurally generated topology. The initial settings become complete playable chapters before the catalog expands further.
