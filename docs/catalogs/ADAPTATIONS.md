# Adaptation web and trait catalog

Current 1.0 design. Shared rules: [SYS-07](../SYSTEMS_BIBLE.md#sys-07-adaptation-and-inherited-expression), lineage integration [SYS-08](../SYSTEMS_BIBLE.md#sys-08-queen-viability-and-lineages). Delivery: [M3/M4](../ROADMAP_1_0.md). Six traits exist; four new traits are planned. This is a biology-inspired composite species, not a literal genetics model.

## Rules family

A trait is an inherited tradeoff with an evidence/eligibility rule, trial investment, compatibility and a small set of permitted physiological hooks. It is not a purchased instantaneous buff. Queueing selects intent; paid laying captures the cohort's immutable bundle; emerging workers add expression through the phenotype ledger. Existing adults do not convert when the web is clicked.

Larval physiology/food costs may apply to a newly laid inheriting cohort when explicitly declared. That does not retroactively modify older brood. Adult effects use actual living carriers, with current expected-carrier presentation including unreturned casualties. Traveling cohorts/parties capture their relevant traits at departure and combine them only when workers physically join.

Preserve Home's accepted worker-trial establishment proxy through its migration. Fertile reproductive groups capture a parent lineage; young queens carry that bundle into their eventual pile and offspring. Daughters gain the same limited trial/queue rules through a paid, explicit lineage path in M4. They must not inherit a trait merely because unrelated immigrant workers express it. No individual genomes, genetic drift or latent-trait reselection in 1.0.

### Definition template

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Catalog/runtime ID, name, family | Stable identity and web grouping; existing runtime IDs below survive migration. |
| Evidence/eligibility | Returned or local experience and existing laying-queen prerequisite. Unlocks are justified by evidence and surviving reproductive/brood progression, not XP points. |
| Trial commitment | Nutrition, nurses, cohort space and time; costs are visible before laying and remain authored. |
| Compatibility | Explicit mutual exclusions and prerequisites. Existing lean/load and security/tolerance exclusions remain. |
| Captured biology | Adult/larval/reproductive applicability, departure snapshots and the denominator for partial expression. |
| Benefit and ongoing cost | A useful response to a stated pressure plus a recurring/physiological disadvantage. No vague “better ants”. |
| Effect whitelist | Named bounded hooks: travel energy/carry, scent decay/deposition, recognition/tending/clearing, combat weight, care efficiency, brood thermal/moisture/feeding response. No arbitrary world mutation. |
| Knowledge projection | Revealed/queued/laid/established/current carrier expression and concise uncertainty about outcomes. No hidden threat strength or guaranteed optimal force. |
| Save/balance evidence | Stable IDs, validated bundles/losses/migrations, useful-pressure and adverse-pressure comparisons. |

| Family | Leaves | Player question |

| Foraging | AT-LEAN / AT-LOAD | Spend less energy per journey or bring more per trip? |
| Chemistry | AT-PERSISTENT / AT-REPAIR | Retain established scent or rebuild it faster under wear/rain? |
| Recognition | AT-SECURITY / AT-TOLERANCE | Detect/clear harmful foreignness sooner or reduce partner-service costs? |
| Combat | AT-MANDIBLES | Pay more to raise workers with greater fighting contribution? |
| Care | AT-NURSES | Invest in care efficiency while accepting its running/raising cost? |
| Environment | AT-HEAT / AT-MOISTURE | Improve a lineage's response to hot or dry brood conditions at a cost in favorable conditions? |

Family hubs organize attention; they are not research purchases. The honeydew relationship can remain a clearly ecological leaf linked to tending; it is not a gene and does not imply route protection. New families appear when locally/collectively justified; hidden locked branches do not list unknown world dangers.

### New 1.0 traits — concrete hooks, uncalibrated magnitudes

**AT-NURSES — Efficient Nurses (Care).** Adult carriers improve the existing brood-care ratio at their pile. The reserve still rounds safely and retains at least the biologically required caregiver presence; trial/reproductive nurses count once. Tradeoff: greater larval feeding cost for inheriting cohorts, paid again as this lineage raises further workers. Eligibility uses observed care pressure and supported brood emergence, not remote truth. Verify useful labor relief versus extra nutrition and mixed-carrier rounding; zero carers never become sufficient.

**AT-HEAT — Heat Resilience (Environment).** Newly laid inheriting brood tolerates a somewhat wider warm Nursery band. It still needs water, care and regulation and can still fail under severe heat. Tradeoff: slower baseline development in comfortable conditions, giving a clear reason not to choose it everywhere. Evidence is local warm-brood strain followed by surviving brood/reproductive progression. Verify captured cohorts under warm and comfortable conditions; never retrofit active brood or grant global outdoor immunity.

**AT-MOISTURE — Moisture Resilience (Environment).** Inheriting brood has a reduced dry-moisture penalty. It does not refill water or erase food demand. Tradeoff: additional larval carbohydrate demand under otherwise favorable conditions. Evidence is local dry-brood strain and surviving progression. Verify recovery under dry pressure versus the nutrition opportunity cost. This is compatible with Heat Resilience but combined costs and maximum mitigation are explicitly bounded.

**AT-REPAIR — Rapid Trail Repair (Chemistry).** Expressed traveling workers lay a stronger replacement contribution where an existing scent corridor is worn or rain-disturbed. It changes deposition, not route familiarity or the decay half-life of old scent. Tradeoff: increased journey food demand. Evidence comes from returned disrupted-trail experience and surviving brood progression. Verify rebuilding versus extra energy; no instant new path, autonomous reroute or hidden weather forecast. It may coexist with Persistent Chemistry; combined food demand and scent strength have authored bounds.

The four new traits are Planned 1.0 M3. Their benefit/cost mechanisms are part of the current design; exact magnitudes remain authored and unset until a card compares meaningful choices. Tuning is not permission to invent another currency or ant caste.

## Presentation and verification

Every leaf explains when it applies, what pays for it, what becomes harder, current expressed carriers and the next queue/lay action. A maximum potential effect must not masquerade as current colony expression. Selecting a node is free attention; queue/replace/cancel and laid/locked are distinct.

Verify each new hook with ordinary mixed cohorts and saved continuations, then one favorable and one adverse comparison. Reuse family checks for ID/compatibility/ledger/projection; do not write separate simulation engines per trait. Maintain trait conservation across actual deaths, emergence, migration and queen founding. M7 checks that no combination becomes a mandatory all-purpose solution.

Post 1.0: individual genomes, latent traits/drift/reselection, detailed mating genetics, large trait trees, distinct soldier caste production and unrelated behavior XP. The ten-leaf release web is expandable through definitions after its rules and tradeoffs work.
