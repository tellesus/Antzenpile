# M8 visual direction and first OUTWARD proof (2026-10-14)

## Player-approved direction
- **Neon is the intended identity**, not a restrained exception. Favor saturated cyan, magenta, violet, lime, amber and coral luminance against dark/near-black fields. Strong visible glowing cores and soft chemical halos are welcome; legibility remains necessary without optional postprocessing.
- Keep OUTWARD a **known-information sensory panorama** and INWARD a functional-organ network; neither becomes an omniscient map. A report may resolve into a **wispy recognizable species silhouette** (spider, beetle, dog/large-animal shape) only after sufficiently supported *returned* species identity. Weak/unconfirmed sensing stays chemically abstract. An older stale report must not refresh a source's live appearance.
- Use flowing smoke masks, bright wisps, layered textured filaments, small glowing ants, stable nonphysical organic paths, and subtle pause-aware real-time animation. Do not generate scenery where the colony has no information.
- On the development workstation (Windows 11 Pro, Ryzen 7 7800X3D, Radeon RX 6650 XT 8 GB VRAM, 64 GB RAM, 1920×1080 240 Hz SDR), establish measured 1080p performance before raising effect counts. **60 FPS** is the first target, not a claimed measurement or a hardware cap; 120 FPS is stretch. Preserve headless and 900×600 input/layout checks. Windows first; do not choose mobile-centric limits at the expense of the desktop aesthetic.
- Retain the Godot 4.7.2 pin and Compatibility renderer until an identical-scene comparison demonstrates a reason to change. SDR glows can use layered low-alpha auras, textured filaments and narrow saturated/near-white cores without HDR 2D.

## Interaction improvements approved for the next UI slice
- Radial treatment where it reduces friction, not for dense data tables. Outward **Gather, Investigate, Defend/Hunt, Reinforce, Recall** must distinguish different real work and the status of workers already away.
- **Selection/editing is free**; dispatch/order is a deliberate operation. But do not hide that distinction behind two ambiguous actions. A recruitment panel should visibly say **Proposed**, **Already sent**, **Available now**, **Recalled from other duties**, **Waiting**, **Committed on ORDER**, with one primary clearly labeled **SEND/ORDER N ANTS** action (and CANCEL/BACK). Do not promise instant arrivals or victory. Preserve existing user-authorized reallocation, funding, exact ledger and return-before-information.
- For ordinary tasks default to a useful suggestion; offer +/-1, +/-5 or presets with clear meanings rather than repeating confusing number/commit controls. If a choice would sacrifice nursery care or reallocate protected work, show the consequence before ORDER. Summarize action feedback as acknowledged/queued/away/returned rather than success before a return report.
- UI review should inspect active `src/presentation/outward/outward_view.gd`, conflict popover, gathering and effort draft implementations first, then conduct controlled mouse/touch usability tests before broad refactor.
