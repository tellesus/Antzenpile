# Archive — historical documents only

**ARCHIVE ONLY.** Documents under this directory are frozen historical records, not current plans, contracts or implementation instructions. Their “next”, “current”, “required” and completion statements apply only to their recorded build. Do not implement an archived recommendation merely because it appears here.

Start at the [current documentation](../README.md), [systems bible](../SYSTEMS_BIBLE.md), [1.0 roadmap](../ROADMAP_1_0.md) or [Current Build](../CURRENT_BUILD.md).

[pre_1_0](pre_1_0/README.md) retains 192 documents from the pre-reset repository: 137 numbered cards, superseded plans/vision, historical technical contracts, reviews/evaluations, art briefs and the old accumulating root overview. Source baseline is commit `44b881f1ae0090a5a2650cd17902610988d073ee`. Archive headers and relative links were added/repaired; underlying historical claims were not promoted to present facts.

Historical raw artifacts stay under `docs/evidence/` because authoring tools/resource paths reference that location. Their status is documented in [evidence guidance](../evidence/README.md); they are evidence, not instructions. Existing user-generated imports/translations/images were not moved or staged.

When useful, cite a specific archived record as dated evidence and reconcile it with current code. Completed future cards may be archived here at a milestone, with a banner, manifest and repaired links. Keep the active roadmap singular.
