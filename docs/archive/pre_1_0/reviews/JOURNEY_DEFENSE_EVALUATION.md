# Journey investigation and intervention evidence — 083–084

Three ordinary Backyard colonies discovered honeydew through paid scouting (standing exploration used when the first scout found another source), suffered returned trail losses, stopped gathering and funded a route survey. Each delivered ambusher report was cloned into intervention, early withdrawal and untouched branches. Intervention dispatched twelve defenders without reinforcement; withdrawal dispatched twelve then recalled after six seconds. All branches reopened five-worker gathering at the same simulated time and ran 180 seconds. No hidden information controlled player choices. Truth access measured casualties and verification only.

All three intervention parties reported success only on home return. All withdrawn parties returned without combat casualties and left the ambusher present. Rival trail workers remained six in every branch: removal affected the manageable ambusher only. This demonstrates a consequential response and harvest recovery; three seeds and a short comparison do not settle balance, and broader rival/seasonal pressures remain independent.
