# Collective exploration evaluation — 2026-10-02

Cards 060–062 replace repeated scout launches with standing colony effort, returned search memory, recurring investigations and physical travel along remembered trails. Return-before-information, eight-agent shared capacity and separate worker commitments remain enforced.

Windows, pinned Godot 4.7.2, Compatibility; [reproducible driver](../../../../tests/evaluate_collective_exploration.gd), [raw evidence](../../../evidence/card062_exploration.json). Nine runs cover three seeds and 960 simulated seconds per run. This duration is a measurement window, not a session-length target. Initial stores/knowledge are unchanged. The common command policy commits five explorers, invests one five-worker route per known basic resource, rechecks on new positive evidence, and pays chamber/brood costs from actual stores. Standing modes queue investigations for reported-depleted sources. The manual comparison issues repeated new-source commands every five seconds when capacity permits; it does not receive automated standing investigations. Standing runs discovered all seven source identities and developed Nursery in all seeds. First brood emerged at 360 seconds in all standing runs; the manual comparison took 644.5 seconds in seed 591. Standing mode needed no repeated launch commands, versus 10–16 manual launches. All nine runs conserved ledger/detail capacity and continued exactly after a mid-run JSON restore at 16×. Standing peak detail was five; manual peak was five or six because existing trail detours share capacity.

Trunks physically extended departure origins and returned their accounts, but this small map does not establish a universal discovery-speed advantage: first water was unchanged by trunks, and standing was slower than manual for seed 482817. Final stores still showed protein/water surplus. No collection-rate sweep, extra funding, storage limit or session target follows from that surplus. The safety policy currently excludes trunks with any returned losses/foreign contact, even when historical; reconsider only with a scoped safety-memory design.
