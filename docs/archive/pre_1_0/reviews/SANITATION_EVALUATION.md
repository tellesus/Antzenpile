# Sanitation measurement — card 064

Windows, Godot 4.7.2 Compatibility; three ordinary seeds, 2,400 simulated seconds each, paired with/without cleanup. No injected resources, knowledge, workers or suppressed threats. Same returned-evidence exploration/gathering/chamber/brood policy; cleanup hires two basic cleaners, pays for Midden when affordable, then reduces to one developed cleaner. Exact JSON continuation verified halfway through all six runs. These command-driven trials are interaction evidence, not human playtests or a session-duration target.

Need appeared around 366s. Every cleanup branch paid 24 carbohydrate / 12 protein / 6 water at 370s and released four excavators 90 seconds later. Peak unresolved refuse stayed below 4.06 units with cleanup; unmanaged colonies spent about 1,197 seconds above the larval slowdown threshold. One developed cleaner handled these populations without straining the nursery. Worker/material conservation held.

Cleanup is not an unconditional population improvement: its food cost competed with growth, and two cleanup branches exhausted carbohydrate under this policy. The policy retains one initially selected source per resource and rechecks from new returned availability; it does not replace poor routes with better newly known sources. This exposes a source-selection/reliability follow-up rather than proving rates or new costs need changing. Broader balance and the original session target remain deferred. No Android or human-playtest claims.
