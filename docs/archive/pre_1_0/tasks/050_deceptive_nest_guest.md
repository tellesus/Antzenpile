# 050 — Deceptive nest guest

Status: complete. Depends on 049. Sources: GDD Part 4 §§70–84, 146, 160.

## Player outcome

One initially tolerated guest enters the home pile, acquires colony odor and consumes occasional brood. Nurses notice losses before they can associate them with internal foreignness. INWARD offers a rejection effort that commits real workers until the guest is purged or the effort is stopped. Preserve uncertainty without a hidden-enemy announcement or a separate management game.

## Executable scope

- Author one aggregate, non-reproducing guest arriving at tick 4800, after the earlier exterior pressures and initial brood/trial cycles. It consumes one immature ant per 240 ticks while brood is present; missing brood never removes adult workers. Grow private chemical integration from zero to one over 1200 ticks of toleration. Values are provisional ecological fixtures, not a session-length target.
- Add conserved brood mortality and monotonic cohort IDs: started brood equals emerged adults + immature deaths + active brood. Partial cohorts mature their actual survivors; a wholly lost adaptation trial releases its nurses without expressing a trait, allowing a new paid trial. Preserve older version-5 snapshots.
- Entry supplies a tolerated guest observation; first observed nursery loss has uncertain cause, and two losses associate internal foreignness with the Nursery. No exact integration, damage schedule or hidden population reaches normal views.
- Once loss evidence exists, Increase Rejection reserves four available workers through an internal ledger commitment. It halts further odor acquisition and clears the guest over 30–60 seconds according to integration at effort start. Damage can continue during that effort. Stop Rejection releases labor; restarting retains completed effort against the same guest. Completion releases labor and records a purged observation. No permanent security slider, false-positive system or mutualist aggression yet.
- Conditional Guest function in INWARD, separate from chamber/adaptation controls; restrained suspected-foreignness cue at Nursery after detection. All commands share mouse/touch semantic paths. Guest state is typed/headless and evidence is explicit.
