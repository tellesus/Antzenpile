# 049 — First rival swarm and response

Status: complete. Depends on 048. Sources: GDD Part 2 §§60–66; Part 4 §§22–35 and 142–143.

## Coherent outcome

Sustained traffic at the known foreign crossing concentrates workers into one stationary aggregate swarm. A returning messenger reports contested traffic; the player can reinforce through the ordinary route or withdraw. Real travel limits reinforcement, battle/retreat changes living ledgers, and returning survivors provide the outcome. This completes the minimal rival test story without a tactical battle screen or full rival strategy AI.

## Boundaries to resolve in implementation

- One stationary junction/swarm at a time, run-owned typed state; references actual player cohorts and the rival's existing route commitment rather than duplicating workers. Compute the crossing from hidden segments. No moving fronts, soldier genomes, nest assault or satellite systems.
- Define a modest sustained-contact trigger, bounded combat interval/loss rule and automatic retreat threshold as visibly provisional fixtures. Seeded group losses reconcile player/rival ledgers, cargo and phenotype; player casualty evidence remains private until home. Separate predator and rival loss history for valid saves.
- Use existing route departures for player reinforcement so added workers cannot teleport into battle. Rival workers likewise arrive through their real route. A ledger-accounted messenger must travel home before exposing a newly formed conflict; loading does not refresh that evidence from hidden state.
- Existing Stop Traffic must end further recruitment, withdraw living participants through real return travel and resolve the swarm. Returned outcome and mortality can remain stale. UI may show a bounded colliding chemical cue and a touch-sized reinforcement action, never true side counts, enemy HP or the rival nest.
- Save forming/fighting/resolved phases, courier/in-flight reinforcement, private casualties and returned outcome. Validate participant ownership, temporal bounds, chemistry, ledger conservation and cause-specific history. Preserve earlier version-5 saves.

## Executable prototype defaults

- After two returned foreign reports, a fresh crossing can form the one stationary swarm. Use true segment intersection for physical ownership; normal art stays on the remembered source. Keep a 240-tick cooldown between swarms.
- Player travelers reaching the junction become held aggregate participants in their existing trail commitment. A one-worker messenger splits from a group when a cohort slot is available, travels home with private contested evidence and cannot report source depletion. Under the eight-cohort cap, the arriving group itself can serve as messenger instead. No population duplication; cargo/debt remain conserved.
- Rival foragers arrive through their existing outbound/inbound route, then pause at the junction; they do not teleport from their pile. When both sides are present, each 16-tick round resolves one seeded casualty, weighted by current group numbers. Select a player casualty cohort by its live share and select adapted loss by the living adapted fraction. Quality/caste combat profiles remain later expansions.
- Automatically withdraw player survivors below a 0.35 force ratio, and rival survivors at two or fewer participants. An explicit Stop Traffic withdraws immediately but releases labor only on home arrival. A returned contested report makes the existing plus button a +4 reinforcement step; those workers still depart/travel normally.
- Preserve separate rival/player cumulative combat casualties and cause-specific private/returned losses alongside existing predator history. Held cohorts resume their captured journey after resolution; survivors report secured/withdrew/dispersed only at home. A wholly lost group reports missing workers without claiming an observed battle outcome.
- These are bounded provisional combat/interaction values, not a new balance target or full warfare system.
