# 055 — Returned loss evidence

Status: complete. Depends on 054. Sources: accepted D26; GDD Part 4 §§59–63.

## Outcome and contract

Returning survivors can distinguish a sudden attack from foreign-ant fighting. A wholly lost cohort supplies only missing-worker evidence at its expected home time. Repeated delivered attack reports identify a risky journey without exposing the predator, precise location, recovery schedule or live casualty. Evidence remains remembered/stale; depletion stays independent.

Record private survivor witness flags at real casualty events and clear them if the party is wholly lost. Deliver optional aggregate report counts/times at home; save/restore validates causal count bounds, survivors and timestamps. Older records default to unknown rather than retroactively revealing causes. Existing worker loss, cargo, travel and response commands remain unchanged.
