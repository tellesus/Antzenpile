Status: ready to scope against current rendering. Depends on 046. Source: GDD Part 7A, especially §§1–8, 23–34.

## Player-visible outcome

Make a discovered source, its active/weakening trail, returning traffic and an alarm feel like a living chemical sensorium. Establish a reusable visual language before adding the next ecology relationships. Preserve the selected controls and compact layout.

## Bounded direction

- Replace object-like resource rings with bounded organic clouds: warm particulate carbohydrate, distinct cyan moisture, violet protein, and a restrained returned red-orange alarm. Confidence/age and reported depletion affect the forms without reading hidden stock or threat geometry.
- Thin bright trail core, faint sparse chemical envelope and capped moving representatives. Motion is presentation-only; pheromone and familiarity remain separate visual inputs. Make a weak chemical trail break/fade while remembered routes can retain faint wisps. Draw no incidental particles in uninformed space.
- Suggest representative ants near Home and on established major flows, without one sprite per worker or implying actual traveler coordinates. Use detached known traffic only. Keep the Home physical suggestion small; OUTWARD stays a rotating panorama.
- Match the INWARD functional palette and selected-context hierarchy without replacing its abstract network. Keep Adaptation distinct from construction. Existing mouse/touch actions remain usable with no hover dependency.
- Restrained sound may reinforce returned reports if a small coherent cue fits the existing audio system. Define its one-shot trigger and reload behavior before implementing it; do not couple audio to simulation state ownership or add a dependency.
- Use current Compatibility drawing, simple curves/particles/masks and reusable authored controls. Concept images guide mood/hierarchy, not pixel fidelity. No requirement for final textures, bloom, literal scenery or a complete UI redesign.
