- Suggest representative ants near Home and on established major flows, without one sprite per worker or implying actual traveler coordinates. Use detached known traffic only. Keep the Home physical suggestion small; OUTWARD stays a rotating panorama.

- Restrained sound may reinforce returned reports if a small coherent cue fits the existing audio system. Define its one-shot trigger and reload behavior before implementing it; do not couple audio to simulation state ownership or add a dependency.
- Use current Compatibility drawing, simple curves/particles/masks and reusable authored controls. Concept images guide mood/hierarchy, not pixel fidelity. No requirement for final textures, bloom, literal scenery or a complete UI redesign.
