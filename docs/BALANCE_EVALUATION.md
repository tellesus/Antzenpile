## Policy and reproducibility

Five scouts start in different directions; every 120 seconds, missing water/protein prompts a wider four-direction search subject to ordinary capacity. Decisions consume detached normal summaries and returned sensory reports. Discovered sources receive three trail workers; returned mortality/foreign chemistry prompts withdrawal. Depleted routes are rechecked no more often than every 90 seconds, without looking up renewal schedules. Baseline means no recognition selection; it still uses the same foraging/chemistry investments. Four rejection workers respond to observed **foreignness**, so uncertain symptoms remain tolerated. Hidden state is used only for measurement, ledger assertions and end-of-run save validation. That policy is one cautious strategy, not an optimized player or automatic gameplay.

## Measured defect and adjustment

The old permanent purge ended guest pressure before recognition emergence in two seeds. Security then paid extra partner labor without later protection benefit. Card 059 now allows a **fresh external guest of the same existing kind** after a provisional 2400-tick/600-second quiet interval following purge. At most one remains present. Cleared guests do not resurrect or reproduce. Each arrival starts new odor, symptoms and rejection work; past brood deaths stay in colony history and cannot authorize rejection of a fresh arrival. The next-arrival schedule stays private. Legacy purged saves begin a fresh quiet interval when restored.

Recognition thresholds are authored alongside the trait's existing costs/effects. No collection quantities, source renewal rates, chamber costs, brood rates or trait fees changed: the resource evidence did not justify a broad sweep.

## Gameplay findings and limits

- The cautious policy made 84–113 rechecks per run, including futile checks of finite sources. Knowledge-based investigation/recheck convenience deserves the next usability review; an exact renewal forecast or hidden automatic refresh would violate the design. This measurement does not justify changing all source rates.
- Protection remains useful to the producer after stopping a dangerous collection route, but the colony receives no honeydew while avoiding that route. Reassigning labor or accepting some traffic risk remains a player choice. More relationship consequences/routing will matter before final mutualism economics can be settled.
- Large colonies accumulated available adults after the two current chamber upgrades. More internal functions and resource uses are still needed before judging long-term population/labor balance. Surplus alone does not justify a storage cap, worker upkeep invented outside the design or a population limit.
- Immediate rejection at the first uncertain symptom can reduce tolerance's detection penalty; a security clearing benefit still remains. Human playtests should compare that approach with waiting for stronger evidence. Beneficial internal guests, false positives and full reproductive lineage selection remain future scope.
