# Replacement music: spacious ambient and restrained downtempo

Research/design196, 2026-10-17. **The player rejects the current score and intrusive195 sound bed, accepts the195 anthill, and names RimWorld's main-menu music as a positive reference.** The current audio assets still exist in the playable build; this document defines a replacement direction and production experiments, not a delivered soundtrack. Technical success in190/195 did not establish listening quality.

## Recommendation

Compose a new original acoustic/electronic ambient score, with a restrained lofi groove available in some arrangements. Produce clean, separate instrumental parts offline; combine quiet sections, fuller sections and genuine rests in the game. Use the approved **Surge XT** for shaped synth voices and evolving pads, approved samples/recordings where musically useful, and **Audacity** for editing and listening preparation. Test three short musical directions before building another large adaptive score. The composition, sound palette and arrangement all start afresh.

A local AI model is a useful alternative production experiment, particularly **ACE-Step1.5**, but it is not yet a verified pipeline on this PC. Its stem-oriented features require a different model configuration from its fastest text-to-song configuration. Do not make the whole replacement depend on that experiment succeeding. A musical instrument rendered from MIDI gives much more exact control over harmony, phrasing, separate parts and transitions than independently prompted audio files.

This recommendation is an engineering/artistic inference from the sources below. It is not evidence that a new composition sounds good. The first acceptance gate is the player's preference among actual musical sketches, including whether each becomes irritating when repeated.

Ludeon's own soundtrack announcement describes Alistair Lindsay's combination of guitar and synthesizer; the official album listing describes space-western music and includes **Entry Screen,3:47**. That supports exploring an organic played voice against spacious electronics, with lofi rhythm as an option rather than imposing a continuous hip-hop beat. [Ludeon announcement](https://ludeon.com/blog/2015/10/rimworld-original-soundtrack-by-alistair-lindsay-released/), [official soundtrack listing](https://store.steampowered.com/app/990430/RimWorld_Soundtrack/).

The browser research tool could not fetch/play the supplied video, including its metadata endpoint. Consequently this report does **not** claim a listening transcription, exact tempo/key/chord progression, timestamp analysis or confirmation that that particular upload is identical to the album entry. The player's stated reference and verified publisher description are the evidence; the proposed palette below is our interpretation. No reference audio was downloaded, sampled, used for model conditioning or incorporated into project assets.

Translate the reference into a testable brief: a small number of expressive voices, room around the notes, a warm organic foreground and a slowly moving electronic background. Explore quiet curiosity and solitude without requiring constant musical busyness. Give the player enough melody to care about, then let it withdraw. These are proposed qualities to audition, not a direction to reproduce Lindsay's melody, arrangement or instrument patches.

## What the current implementation tells us

These are source-code observations, not claims of having heard the exported mixes:

- [Current generator](../tools/music/compose_colony_music.py) renders one32-bar,90-BPM arrangement, about85.33seconds. It uses simple synthesized pads/bass plus transposed harp, marimba and percussion samples. The base contains a bass drum in every bar. Circular delayed copies supply the room effect.
- [Current audio intent](../src/audio/music_state.gd) mostly adds Food Exchange, Nursery and Midden voices after development. Once these states persist, the mix can stay full. Brief focus changes adjust their gains; they do not supply a new section, a real rest or a different musical thought.
- [Current controller](../src/audio/audio_controller.gd) starts four persistent loops and moves their gains over three real seconds. It has no musical phrase scheduler. Equal file length and coincident starts help alignment but do not by themselves make an entrance musically appropriate.
- [The195 ambience recipe](../tools/art/entrance195/compose_ambience.py) overlaps70 processed leaf grains into24seconds. Its peak/RMS and loop seam are bounded. Those measures did not predict the player's response: the result was more intrusive. Low amplitude is not a sufficient test of texture, repeated transients or fatigue.

Likely contributors are the palette, repeated attacks, permanent instrumental accumulation and short recognizable recurrence. That is a diagnosis to test, not proof that any particular frequency or instrument caused the rejection. A quieter version of the same score would leave these structural choices intact. Retire its musical content and the rejected rustle bed from the replacement; retain only useful non-musical contracts such as volume preferences, approved-data boundaries, returned-cue eligibility and shutdown cleanup. Information cues need their own listening pass and should not be treated as implicitly accepted.

Surge's manual documents filters, envelopes, modulation, effects and separately routable scene outputs. Its FAQ explicitly permits distributing/selling music made with the synth and factory patches. This makes it a practical source of warm keys, rounded bass, physical-model plucks and slowly modulated pads without shipping a synthesizer in the game. [Surge manual](https://surge-synthesizer.github.io/manual-xt/), [output rights FAQ](https://surge-synthesizer.github.io/faq/).

Surge also exposes a Python rendering interface in its own repository. The documented Windows route builds a native `surgepy` extension; it is not a verified one-command package installation here. The CLI's existence is not proof that it exports a MIDI arrangement to finished stems. Evaluate the native binding as a bounded authoring experiment, with CPU-only synthesis and no inference GPU dependency. Pin the source/build/patches if adopted. [Official build documentation](https://github.com/surge-synthesizer/surge#python-bindings), [official Python examples](https://github.com/surge-synthesizer/surge-python).

The alternative is a dedicated MIDI/audio workstation. **Waveform Free** is a concrete candidate: the publisher lists Windows support, audio/MIDI production and VST3 hosting. Only its free core would be considered; paid expansions are outside scope. It was not in194's approved list, so request approval before adoption. Audacity is useful for finished audio editing, but its documented Note Tracks have limited functionality; do not assume it replaces an instrument-hosting MIDI arrangement workflow. [Waveform Free](https://www.tracktion.com/products/waveform-free), [Audacity Note Tracks](https://manual.audacityteam.org/man/note_tracks.html).

For an important guitar foreground, audition an actual articulated guitar source rather than assume a physical-model synth can impersonate a guitarist. The publisher currently lists **Ample Guitar M Lite** under free software; it is a candidate requiring separate tool/sample-license review and player approval, not an installed or approved dependency. Avoid expanding the tool list until the first palette test demonstrates a need. [Publisher download list](https://www.amplesound.net/en/download.asp). Freesound remains approved for individually checked CC0 recordings, but unrelated complete guitar clips will not automatically share our harmony and tempo.

### Local AI: promising, with specific limits

ACE-Step's repository distinguishes base, SFT and turbo models. Its published table gives **Extract/Lego/Complete** to the base variants, not the SFT/turbo variants. Therefore a fast full-song demo is not evidence of editable synchronized instrumental parts. Our trial would compare a full instrumental sketch with a single reusable part, then check tempo stability, measure phrase boundaries and audition artifacts exposed by muting. The repository is MIT; the official base model card is also marked MIT and states commercial output use is intended. Pin the exact model and all required checkpoints at adoption; these provider statements do not independently establish originality of any particular output. [Repository/model matrix](https://github.com/ace-step/ACE-Step-1.5), [base model card](https://huggingface.co/ACE-Step/acestep-v15-base).

The workstation measured in195 has a Ryzen7800X3D and RX6650XT. Do not change drivers, introduce WSL, rent compute or conduct large sweeps for this research card. A later approved trial should use one short instrumental generation and a stop criterion before downloading more models. ## Musical brief to audition

These are **proposed starting values**, not researched universal genre rules or accepted gameplay contracts.

| Element | Pilot direction | What to listen for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pace | Start around68–76BPM,4/4; one shared tempo for related sections. Include an effectively beatless arrangement. | Unhurried movement without a constant attention-demanding pulse. |
| Foreground | One warm plucked/played voice; soft electric keys as an alternative. Restrained phrase, then several bars of space. | Character and touch rather than a recurring notification-like figure. |
| Harmony | Slowly voiced extended/suspended chords, common tones and limited bass movement; write transitions deliberately. | Warmth without harmonic mud, forced sweetness or constant unresolved tension. |
| Rhythm | Optional soft kick/rim/brush pattern. Consistent laid-back timing and velocity shapes; sparse hats. | Groove through coherent placement, not arbitrary timing jitter. |
| Pad | Slow attack, damped high end and small timbral evolution; long spaces can carry just this layer. | Motion without hiss, beating fatigue or an unbroken wall of sound. |
| Texture | Start without the leaf loop, vinyl crackle or added noise. Add a texture only if its absence demonstrably hurts the piece. | No scratching, repeated rustles or high-frequency distraction. |
| Arrangement | Three related sections with different density, short motifs, deliberate omissions and a breath section. | A sense of a piece developing, rather than a longer loop containing more notes. |

Native Instruments' producer tutorial discusses relaxed tempo, extended chords, muted drums, timing and restrained arrangement. iZotope's groove tutorial explains systematic swing/syncopation. Those are useful production principles; their paid instruments/subscriptions are not proposed purchases. The brief above intentionally does not adopt every lofi convention: noise and degradation are not prerequisites for the player's preferred mood. [Lofi production tutorial](https://blog.native-instruments.com/lo-fi-hip-hop-beats/), [groove tutorial](https://www.izotope.com/community/blog/swing-and-syncopation-understanding-daw-groove).

Ableton's artist-led S1gns Of L1fe tutorial describes layered ambient production with controlled variation and evolving timbres. Disasterpeace's Mini Metro postmortem describes restrained sonification and early prototypes against game footage. The transferable lesson is to test musical feel in context and tightly bound variation. Antzenpile must retain its own independent simulation clock and information boundaries; Mini Metro's coupling between audio and simulation is not an architecture proposal for this game. [Ambient production example](https://www.ableton.com/en/blog/made-in-ableton-live-s1gns-of-l1fe/), [composer postmortem](https://disasterpeace.com/blog/tag.minimalism.sound%2Bdesign).

## Keeping layers and blending without permanent accumulation

Use two forms of change together: **change the musical section over time**, and **adjust a few compatible parts within that section**. The proposed section families are an airy opening, a gentle groove, a warmer/full arrangement and a sparse breath. The musical foreground can exit while the bed continues; developed chambers do not each earn a permanently playing instrument. This is a proposed presentation policy, not a new gameplay reward or hidden-world signal.

Separate parts by musical role: harmonic/organic foreground, pad/space, bass and soft rhythm. Render every part for one section against exactly the same tempo map, chord schedule, start sample and end frame. Silence inside a part remains in the file. A bass part for sectionA is not interchangeable with sectionB merely because both are in the same key. Label compatible transitions and use common-tone or authored bridge bars where needed.

Proposed behavior: remain in an eligible arrangement for at least32–64bars before considering a variation, change at phrase boundaries, and allow long intervals with no foreground or percussion. Start by testing these rules in an offline listening mix. Presentation randomness has its own seed/state and never advances gameplay RNG. Approved local/returned information may gently bias an eligible arrangement after existing information has been communicated; avoid one-note-per-worker sonification or immediate alarming music for hidden contact. Ordinary activity can also remain musically quiet.

Example at72BPM:8bars last26.67seconds. A planned two-bar fade lasts6.67seconds;4bars last13.33seconds. These are calculations, not fixed acceptance targets. Trigger harmony/rhythm changes on a compatible boundary, preserve the musical clock, and allow room/reverb tails to finish. Real time rather than1×/64× simulation speed controls the score. Pause/load/end-review behavior needs an explicit implementation decision using the existing lifecycle requirements.

Godot already supplies useful pieces: **AudioStreamSynchronized** starts substreams together and exposes per-stream gain; **AudioStreamInteractive** supports clip transitions at beat/bar/end and configurable fades. Its same-position transition can help related arrangements of equal length. This makes built-in resources worth evaluating before adding middleware. Do not assume resource nesting proves runtime phase, looping or per-playback gain behavior. [Synchronized stream](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/classes/class_audiostreamsynchronized.html), [interactive stream](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/classes/class_audiostreaminteractive.html).

The pinned4.7.2 engine was probed in196: both classes and the Ogg stream class exist; setting synchronized-stream gain to−80dB round-trips; an interactive resource can contain a synchronized resource. [API evidence](evidence/card196/engine-api.json). **No new audio was played and no transition, nested playback, phase accuracy or musical quality was validated.** The next implementation gate must exercise actual mixer output and packed Windows playback. Avoid relying on frame timers alone for sample-accurate boundaries: Godot documents the distinction between gameplay time, mixer timing and audible output. [Audio synchronization guidance](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/tutorials/audio/sync_with_audio.html).

## Production and mixing requirements

Render dry stems from one arrangement session with identical frame ranges. Preserve editable notes, patch states, source hashes, processing and the full reference mix. Use a shared linear room/reverb return or consistent baked processing so changing voices does not restart the apparent room. If bus compression/saturation depends on the sum, isolated exports may no longer reconstruct the mastered mix; either bake carefully designed arrangement mixes or keep nonlinear processing under a measured common runtime bus. Do not independently normalize every stem.

Judge loudness at the **combined arrangement**, including incoming/outgoing overlap and information cues. Preserve headroom before limiting. An equal-power crossfade of identical correlated material can increase amplitude: at its midpoint0.707+0.707≈1.414, about+3dB. Thus crossfade curves need measurement, not a universal equal-power default. Also test partial layer combinations: a quiet mix can become oddly exposed or unpleasant when a masking part disappears.

Measure integrated and short-term loudness, true peak, transient prominence, low-frequency buildup, stereo/mono compatibility and exposed loop/transition boundaries. Loudness-match A/B/C previews so the loudest sketch does not win by default. Do not claim a universal streaming/broadcast LUFS target applies to the game. A low-level mix can still be fatiguing; player listening is the quality gate.

Consider Ogg for longer music after testing exact imported timing, transitions and CPU cost. Keep lossless production masters. Do not indiscriminately multiply full-length PCM loops: four stereo44.1kHz16-bit parts for180seconds already require about121MiB before decode/runtime overhead, calculated from4×180×44100×2×2bytes. Prefer a small curated section set and a bounded number of concurrent decoders. Compression is a delivery choice after musical approval, not a substitute for an arrangement.

## Next bounded pilot and acceptance

1. **Make three original60–90second sketches**: A, spacious organic foreground with pads, closest to the stated RimWorld direction; B, warm keys and a very restrained lofi groove; C, organic/electronic hybrid with the beat withdrawing for a long breath. Use new phrases/palette, consistent listening loudness and no195 rustle bed. Render a short preview plus an extended repeated version. The reference is a mood guide; no melody copying or source-audio conditioning.
2. **Player selects/rejects musical content first.** Ask which sound feels right, which part becomes annoying, whether the beat is welcome and whether the music can sit behind decisions. Include an option to reject all three. Numerical checks do not override that result. A3–5minute repeated audition can reject a palette early; a later20–30minute play session tests recurrence/fatigue, not a scientific comfort guarantee.
3. **Turn the chosen piece into one small adaptive suite.** Export four musical roles for a few compatible sections plus a sparse/rest variant. Supply one demonstration mix with explicitly timed entrances, exits, a section change and cue overlap. Check mix reconstruction, phrase boundaries, all supported combinations, tails and overlap headroom.
4. **Integrate only after that sounds convincing.** Replace the rejected four-stem score and195 ambience rather than layering the new suite over them. Keep independent preferences and approved returned-cue rules. Exercise pause/load/review/quit and1×/64× behavior, actual native mixer timing and Windows package. Full headless regression follows final runtime changes. The player then judges the actual game mix.

Waveform Free, Ample Guitar M Lite, ACE-Step and a separator are **researched candidates only**, not newly authorized tools.
